# Average of Odd and Even Elements

## Problem

In an array of elements, find the average of odd elements and the average of even elements, and display the results.

## Test Case

**Number of test cases:** `3`

### Input

```text
10 2 19 27 16 3
3 9 5 15
12 46 20 14 26
```

### Output

```text
T1

Even avg: 9.33

Odd avg: 16.33

T2

Even avg: 0.00

Odd avg: 8.00

T3

Even avg: 23.60

Odd avg: 0.00
```
